Playlist Cleaner

Find and remove duplicate songs from your YouTube playlists, right from your iPhone.

What Playlist Cleaner does

Playlist Cleaner is an iOS app for people who manage YouTube playlists and end up with the same song added more than once. After signing in with your Google Account, the app lets you:

  • View all of your YouTube playlists and how many videos are in each one
  • Scan any playlist to automatically detect duplicate videos
  • Remove the duplicate entries with a single tap, keeping the original
  • Search YouTube and add a video to an existing playlist, or create a new playlist on the spot

The app is a lightweight client for the official YouTube Data API — it has no backend server of its own, and your playlist data is never stored anywhere except temporarily in the app while it's running.
